Dress code02

Shoes are welcome; sandals are permitted for women. Shirts, polos and hoodies are permitted, along with sleeveless tops for women. No flip-flops, slippers or slides. No sandos, tank tops or jerseys for men.

Table requests and private event packages are handled by The Zone team. Celebration food packages start at 50 guests, with exclusive venue use. Events of 100 guests or more include a DJ.
